# Family Trip — Destinations & Things to Do

*Companion to the logistics document. Same route order: Munich → Rettenberg (Allgäu) → Chur → Bernina railway → Varenna (Lake Como) → Venice → Milan.*

---

## Munich (optional arrival night)

The gentle landing after long-haul flights.
- **Marienplatz & the Glockenspiel** — the old-town heart; the carillon plays at 11:00 and 12:00.
- **A classic beer hall evening** — Hofbräuhaus for the famous one, Augustiner Bräustuben for the local's choice. Pretzels, roast pork, litre steins.
- **Viktualienmarkt** — the food market, perfect for a first-morning wander before picking up the car.
- **English Garden** (if energy allows) — watch the river surfers at the Eisbach wave.

## Rettenberg & the Allgäu (2–3 nights)

Bavaria's alpine dairy country: green foothills, onion-domed churches, and the Alps rising straight ahead. Parents are with their friends; the cyclists ride.

**For the cyclists (e-bikes rented locally):**
- **Iller valley ride to Oberstdorf and back** (~50–55 km, flat valley path, mountains in view the whole way) — the signature day. Extend at the turnaround with the **Breitachklamm gorge** walk or a cable car up the **Nebelhorn** or **Fellhorn**.
- **Lakes day** — Großer Alpsee at Immenstadt, Niedersonthofener See, or Rottachsee: rolling terrain, swimmable in early September (~40–50 km).
- **Kleinwalsertal** — ride through Oberstdorf into a dead-end Austrian alpine valley reachable only from Germany; steady climb where the e-bike shines.
- **Bad Hindelang / Oberjoch direction** — bigger climbs, bigger views.

**For everyone:**
- **Rettenberg is Germany's southernmost brewery village** — home to Zötler (claims to be Germany's oldest family-run brewery, brewing since 1447) and Engelbräu. Brewery tours and beer gardens on the doorstep.
- **Grünten** — "the guardian of the Allgäu," the local mountain, for walks and viewpoints.
- Cheese country: alpine dairies (Sennereien) sell Bergkäse direct.

**Day trips by car (while we still have it):**
- **Neuschwanstein & Hohenschwangau castles (Füssen, ~1h15):** the fairy-tale castle. Book timed interior tickets **well in advance online** — September walk-ups usually fail. The exterior views and the Marienbrücke bridge viewpoint are the real highlight; pair with lunch in pretty Füssen.
- **Wieskirche (~30 min beyond Füssen):** UNESCO rococo pilgrimage church standing alone in a meadow — a 45-minute jaw-dropper, natural add-on to the castle day.
- **Lindau old town:** the historic harbor island on Lake Constance — we drive here anyway to drop the car, so arrive early and enjoy the harbor and lanes before the train.
- **Bregenz & the Pfänder cable car (Austria, ~40 min):** panoramic ride above Lake Constance — a good non-castle alternative.

## Chur, Switzerland (1 night)

Switzerland's oldest town (5,000 years of settlement), tucked in the Rhine valley.
- **Car-free Altstadt** — an evening stroll through lanes, courtyards, and the cathedral quarter.
- **Chur Cathedral** (12th-century, hilltop bishop's court district).
- Dinner in the old town — Graubünden specialties: capuns, pizzokel, Bündnerfleisch.
- Early night — the great train leaves in the morning.

## The Bernina Railway (travel day, but a headline attraction)

A UNESCO World Heritage line and the highest rail crossing of the Alps — glaciers, spiral viaducts, and turquoise lakes from your seat.
- **Landwasser Viaduct** — the iconic curved bridge into a cliff-face tunnel.
- **Albula spirals** — the line corkscrews inside the mountain to gain height.
- **Morteratsch Glacier** and the Bernina massif — permanent snow and ice in view even in September; the valleys still green, larches turning gold late in the month.
- **Ospizio Bernina (2,253 m)** — the summit, beside the milky-turquoise **Lago Bianco**.
- **Alp Grüm** — balcony view down toward Italy.
- **Brusio circular viaduct** — the train loops over itself descending into the vineyards.
- Arrival in **Tirano**, Italy — lunch stop; try **pizzoccheri**, the Valtellina buckwheat-pasta dish.

## Varenna, Lake Como (4 nights)

The quiet eastern-shore alternative to famous Bellagio: a small fishing village of pastel houses, largely car-free, calm in the evenings after day-trippers leave. The base for the slow middle of the trip.

- **The ferry triangle** — Varenna ↔ **Bellagio** ↔ **Menaggio** by lake ferry: visit the famous village without sleeping in its crowds. The mid-lake ferries are the region's transport *and* its best sightseeing.
- **Villa Monastero** (Varenna) — lakefront botanical gardens along a kilometer of shoreline.
- **Castello di Vezio** — ruined castle above Varenna; short uphill walk, falconry displays, the best panorama of the lake's three branches.
- **Passeggiata degli Innamorati** ("Lovers' Walk") — the clifftop lakeside promenade into the village.
- **Villa Carlotta** (Tremezzo, across the water) — the grand villa-and-gardens showpiece, reached by ferry.
- **Fiumelatte** — one of Italy's shortest rivers, a foaming white cascade, a short walk south.
- Unstructured day: market morning, long waterfront lunch, optional private boat hire on the lake, swim from the lido.
- Food: lake fish (missoltini, lavarello), Valtellina wines from the terraced vineyards just up the valley.

## Venice (3 nights)

The only city of its kind on earth — canals for streets, boats for buses. Crucially: **nobody drives here**, so every visitor moves the same way, on foot and by vaporetto. Three nights means seeing Venice at its best — early mornings and evenings after the day-trippers leave.

- **Piazza San Marco** — the Basilica's golden mosaics (book ahead), the **Doge's Palace**, and the Campanile lift for the lagoon view. Go early.
- **Grand Canal by vaporetto Line 1** — the world's greatest main street, best ridden at dusk from a front seat.
- **Rialto** — the bridge, and the **Rialto fish & produce market** in the morning.
- **Get lost on purpose** — Cannaregio and Castello backstreets, where actual Venetians live.
- **Bacaro crawl** — Venice's signature evening: cicchetti (small bites) and ombre (small wines) standing at counters, hopping bar to bar.
- **Gondola or traghetto** — the full gondola if it appeals; the €2 traghetto ferry crossing as the local's version.
- **Island day** — **Murano** (glass furnaces) and **Burano** (the rainbow-painted fishing village, lace, photogenic beyond reason) by vaporetto.
- **Accademia** or the **Peggy Guggenheim Collection** for art; **La Fenice** opera house tours.
- Sunset from the **Zattere** promenade or a rooftop terrace, spritz in hand.

## Milan (1 night — the farewell)

A working fashion-and-finance city with a handful of world-class sights: perfect for one strong final evening and morning.

- **The Duomo** — climb (or lift) to the **rooftop terraces** and walk among the marble spires; late afternoon light is best.
- **Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II** — the 19th-century glass arcade beside the Duomo; aperitivo with a view of it.
- **The Last Supper (Cenacolo)** — Leonardo's masterpiece. **Must be booked weeks in advance**; if secured, it is the single best 15 minutes in Milan.
- **Brera district** — cobbled lanes, galleries, and the trip's farewell dinner.
- **Navigli canals** — evening aperitivo scene along the water, if energy remains.
- Morning of departure: coffee and a last stroll, then the 50-minute Malpensa Express. Calm exit.

---

## The trip in one line

Bavarian alps and family time → the highest railway over the Alps → four slow days on Italy's most beautiful lake → three days in the world's most extraordinary city → one grand farewell in Milan.
