# Advance Bookings & Pre-Trip Checks — September 2027

*The single list of everything that must be booked or verified ahead of time. Ordered by when to act. Check things off as they're done.*

---

## As soon as dates are fixed

- [ ] **Flights** — SIN → MUC (parents), PHL → MUC (X & E); home from MXP. Before booking the return, compare flying home from VCE instead (would cut the Milan night — decide deliberately).
- [ ] **Rental car, Munich → Lindau (domestic one-way).** ⚠️ Before locking the departure day: confirm the Lindau branch (a) accepts one-way returns and (b) its **opening hours** — small-town German branches often close Saturday midday and all day Sunday. This is the hidden constraint that can force a different departure day. No sightseeing planned in Lindau: it's car drop → train.
- [ ] **Bernina Express panoramic seats** (Chur → Tirano, morning departure). Reservation ~CHF 26–32 pp on top of fare; **September sells out** — book the day dates are set. Fallback needing no reservation: hourly Rhaetian Railway regionals on the identical line.
- [ ] **Hotels** — Chur (1), Iseo town (4 — lakefront, near the ferry pier), Venice (3 — near a vaporetto stop, no bridge-heavy walk with luggage), Milan (1), optional Munich arrival night.

## ~2–3 months out

- [ ] **Tirano → Iseo private transfer (NCC).** ⚠️ *Easiest to forget and hardest to fix on the day.* Get 2–3 quotes (~€150–250/car) from Valtellina/Sondrio NCC operators, or ask the Iseo hotel to arrange it. Confirm: pickup at Tirano station square, luggage for 4, Aprica Pass photo stop. Budget fallback if it falls through: bus Tirano → Edolo + train Edolo → Iseo (verify timetables — rural and infrequent).
- [ ] **The Last Supper, Milan.** Sells out weeks-to-months ahead; slots release in batches — check the release calendar and set a reminder. If missed, official guided-tour resellers sometimes hold allocations.
- [ ] **Neuschwanstein interior tickets** (timed entry, hohenschwangau.de). September slots sell out ~2–3 weeks ahead or more; plan to be in Hohenschwangau ~1.5–2 h before the slot.
- [ ] **Franciacorta cellar visit** (Ca' del Bosco or Berlucchi). September = harvest — tours fill; book the day around it, with a vineyard lunch.

## ~1 month out

- [ ] **Frecciarossa tickets** (Brescia → Venezia S. Lucia; Venezia → Milano Centrale). Advance = cheaper, but the cheap fares are train-specific — **pick a Brescia departure that leaves ≥45–60 min after the regional arrives** (i.e., take the Iseo → Brescia regional one departure earlier than the tightest fit). See transfer-buffer note in trip-logistics.
- [ ] **Swiss train legs via SBB** (Lindau → Chur). No reservation needed; saver fares exist if dates are certain.
- [ ] **E-bikes, Rettenberg area** (~€35–45/day; Rettenberg, Sonthofen, Fischen, Oberstdorf). Reserve for September.
- [ ] **San Marco Basilica timed entry** (Venice); optional: Doge's Palace, Campanile, La Fenice tour.
- [ ] **Venice overnight pre-registration** — the day-tripper access fee doesn't apply to overnight guests, but there's a free pre-registration; the hotel helps. Ask when confirming the room.

## The week before / on the road

- [ ] **Marienbrücke status** (hohenschwangau.de) — the bridge viewpoint is the main reason for the castle day and has a history of temporary maintenance/weather closures. If closed, consider swapping the castle day for Bregenz/Pfänder.
- [ ] **Trenord "cantieri" (engineering works) page** for the Brescia–Iseo–Edolo line — this branch periodically runs replacement buses, which would change the Venice travel-day timings.
- [ ] **Reconfirm the NCC transfer** 2–3 days before the Bernina day.
- [ ] **Exact Bernina departure time** for the date (nominally ~8:15 Chur → ~12:45 Tirano — verify).
- [ ] **Vaporetto multi-day passes** — buy on arrival in Venice (no advance booking needed; listed here so nobody queues per-ride).

## Deliberately NOT booked in advance

- Lake Iseo ferries (turn up and go), Monte Isola bikes, Duomo rooftop (book 1–2 days ahead is fine), restaurants except the Franciacorta lunch and the Milan farewell dinner (book those with the respective visits).
