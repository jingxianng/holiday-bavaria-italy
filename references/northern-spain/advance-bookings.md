# Advance Bookings & Pre-Trip Checks — Early September

*The single list of everything that must be booked or verified ahead of time. Ordered by when to act. Check things off as they're done.*

⚠️ **Flex-night rule:** we may still move one night (Madrid +1 *or* Bilbao +1 — **Bilbao is the current lean**, adding a Getaria + Zumaia day — funded by Cantabria −1, which deletes the spare/weather-insurance day). **Book everything flexible/refundable until that decision is locked.**

---

## As soon as dates are fixed

- [ ] **Flights** — PHL → MAD and BIO → MAD → PHL (X & E; if ticketed separately for price, keep a 3 h+ MAD connection and pack carry-on-friendly). Parents: SIN → MAD inbound; outbound BIO → MAD, **airport-hotel night at MAD**, MAD → SIN next day — book the hotel with the flights.
- [ ] **Rental car, Oviedo → Bilbao airport (one-way).** ⚠️ **Confirm the one-way fee** when booking — don't assume. Automatic transmission sells out in Spain — book early if needed. Full-size/estate for 4 + luggage. Confirm the BIO airport drop and the Oviedo pickup location/hours — **preferred plan: pick the car up the evening before the transfer day** (small overnight-parking cost buys an 8:00 start on the big day).
- [ ] **Hotels — all refundable until the flex-night decision:** Madrid (3; Sol–Huertas–Retiro area), Oviedo (2; old town), Cantabria (4; **Comillas or San Vicente de la Barquera if sea views matter, Santillana del Mar otherwise**), Bilbao (3; **must have parking**), parents' MAD airport (1).
- [ ] **Renfe sale-date reminder** — Madrid → Oviedo Alvia tickets release in waves a few months out; book the **~3h15 fast service from Chamartín** when it appears. (Segovia AVE stays unbooked — it's a game-time decision unless the flex night makes it a committed day.)

## ~Early spring (season plan release)

- [ ] **Alsa shuttle, Lakes of Covadonga.** The lakes road is closed to private cars June–mid-October and the shuttle from Cangas de Onís bus station is the way up. **The season's operating plan/timetable publishes around early spring — verify the exact dates and booking window then, and book online as soon as it opens.** Plan: park at the Cangas bus-station lot, lakes first, basilica after, lunch in Cangas.

## ~2–3 months out

- [ ] **Prado** timed entry — **opening slot**; the visit is a 90-minute highlights run.
- [ ] **Guggenheim Bilbao** timed entry. ⚠️ **Traditionally closed Mondays outside high summer — verify for our dates.** With the Sunday arrival this fixes the order: **Urdaibai coast loop Monday (Gernika market day!), Guggenheim Tuesday.**
- [ ] **Sobrino de Botín, Madrid** — the one booked restaurant in Madrid; reserves out well ahead.
- [ ] **Traditional Bilbao asador/fish house** — the splurge whole-turbot-over-coals dinner; pick and book with the hotel dates.
- [ ] **Gaztelugatxe free timed permit** — for the Monday Urdaibai loop; book once the date is fixed.
- [ ] **Getaria grill house** — ⚠️ **only if the flex night lands in Bilbao** (it's the encore day with Zumaia); book then, not before.
- [ ] **Altamira museum** (optional) — book only if the group opts in on the coast day.

## ~1 month out

- [ ] **Lock the flex night** (Madrid vs Bilbao vs keep Cantabria at 4) and convert refundable holds to firm bookings.
- [ ] Reconfirm the rental car (one-way fee, automatic), the Bilbao hotel parking, and the Alsa shuttle times for our exact date.

## The week before / on the road

- [ ] **Covadonga morning weather** — bad morning = skip the lakes (no shuttle into cloud); basilica briefly or straight to the coast. Fuente Dé later in the week is the mountain backup.
- [ ] **Fuente Dé webcam + mountain forecast** each Cantabria morning — clear = cable car + Potes; cloud = Santander. Decided at breakfast, never in advance.
- [ ] **Gulpiyuri tide** (if stopping) — go near high tide; it's a sandpit at low.
- [ ] **Madrid airport taxi flat fare** — verify the current fixed rate (~€33 historically).
- [ ] **Transfer-day packing drill** — luggage loaded in Oviedo, trunk not reopened until the Cantabria hotel; valuables/meds/passports in daypacks that leave the car at every stop.

## Deliberately NOT booked in advance

- Segovia (game-time), sidrerías in Oviedo (walk in — it's the culture), pintxos bars in Bilbao (grazing is the point), Artxanda funicular, the Vizcaya Bridge gondola (turn up, under €1), El Rastro, Retiro, all beaches, Santoña, Santander, and every meal not named above. Exactly enough reservations to remove stress, and not one more.
