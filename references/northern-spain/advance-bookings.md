# Advance Bookings & Pre-Trip Checks — Early September (year TBD)

*The single list of everything that must be booked or verified ahead of time. Ordered by when to act. Check things off as they're done.*

---

## As soon as dates are fixed

- [ ] **Flights** — PHL → MAD and BIO → MAD → PHL (X & E; if ticketed separately for price, keep a 3 h+ MAD connection and pack carry-on-friendly). Parents: SIN → MAD inbound; outbound BIO → MAD, **airport-hotel night at MAD**, MAD → SIN next day — book the hotel with the flights so the layover plan is locked.
- [ ] **Rental car, Oviedo → Bilbao airport (domestic one-way).** Drop fee expected ~€50–150 — get quotes, don't assume. ⚠️ **Automatic transmission sells out in Spain — book early if needed.** Full-size/estate for 4 + luggage. Confirm the BIO airport drop and the Oviedo city pickup location/hours.
- [ ] **Hotels** — Madrid (3; Sol–Huertas–Retiro area), Oviedo (2; old town), Llanes (2), Comillas *or* Santillana del Mar (3; pick on lodging quality), Bilbao (2; **must have parking** — the car stays with us), parents' MAD airport (1).
- [ ] **Renfe sale-date reminder** — Madrid → Oviedo Alvia tickets are released in waves a few months out; set a reminder to check rather than assuming a window. Book the **~3h15 fast service from Chamartín** when it appears. Same day: the **Segovia AVE** day-trip tickets (cheap, but timed).

## ~2–3 months out

- [ ] **Prado** timed entry (opening slot, not the free evening hours).
- [ ] **Royal Palace** timed entry; **Reina Sofía** (for *Guernica*) — quick to book, easy to forget.
- [ ] **Guggenheim Bilbao** timed entry.
- [ ] **Altamira Museum** tickets (the Neocueva replica — the real-cave lottery is not a plan).
- [ ] **Getaria grill house** (harbor *parrilla* — whole turbot). These tables book out; reserve the day the Basque-coast day is fixed.
- [ ] **Sobrino de Botín, Madrid** (if wanted) — the 1725 roast-house books well ahead.
- [ ] **Tito Bustillo cave, Ribadesella** (optional) — tiny daily quota, books out fast; skip guilt-free since Altamira covers cave art.
- [ ] **Gaztelugatxe free timed permit** (if that option wins over Gernika/Zumaia) — required in season; book once the day is fixed.

## ~1 month out

- [ ] **Covadonga lakes access rules for our dates** — in high season private cars are banned daytime and a shuttle runs from Cangas de Onís; early-September weekends likely still restricted. Plan the day (and its start time) around the answer.
- [ ] **Segovia lunch** — book a *cochinillo* house (e.g. one of the classic asadores) for the day-trip date.
- [ ] Reconfirm the rental car (automatic!), and the Bilbao hotel's parking arrangement.

## The week before / on the road

- [ ] **Fuente Dé webcam + mountain forecast** each morning in Cantabria — clear = cable-car day, cloud = Santander/Altamira day. This is a decision made at breakfast, not in advance.
- [ ] **Bufones de Pría** — only worth the detour with a decent swell and mid-to-high tide; check conditions before leaving Llanes.
- [ ] **Gulpiyuri beach** — go near high tide (it's a sandpit at low).
- [ ] **Madrid airport taxi flat fare** — verify the current fixed rate to the center (~€33 historically) so nobody haggles at midnight.

## Deliberately NOT booked in advance

- Sidrerías in Oviedo and Llanes (walk in — it's the culture), pintxos bars in Bilbao (grazing is the point), Artxanda funicular, El Rastro, Retiro, all beaches and viewpoints, and every meal not named above. The trip should have exactly enough reservations to remove stress, and not one more.
